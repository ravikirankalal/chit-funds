Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you give us when you ask for a callback or a quotation on this website.

What we collect

  • What you type into the form: your name and phone number, and optionally your email, city, wedding date, guest count, preferred call time, the services you are interested in, and any notes.
  • Which advertisement or link brought you here (for example the campaign name), so we know which ads work.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, a Google account identifier so you can see your own requests. We do not receive your Google password.

Why we use it

Only to call or message you about your wedding, to send you a quotation, and to understand which advertising brings enquiries. We do not sell your details or share them with other businesses for their marketing.

Who can see it

Our planner can see and manage the requests. Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us. You can see only your own requests, and only if you are signed in.

Advertising and measurement

If we run Google Ads, the site may load Google's tag to count that an enquiry was made. It records that a request happened, not the contents of your form.

How long we keep it

We keep a request while we are working on your enquiry, and remove it when you ask us to or when we no longer need it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, tell us when we call, or use the phone or WhatsApp number shown on the site. We will act on your request promptly.
